Reading the years. Financial figures come from the Form 10-K and environmental figures from a separate sustainability report. The two are published on different cycles, so a company's emissions year usually trails its financial year. Each cell states the year it belongs to — a row is not a single period. Scope 2 is market-based where a company reports both. An empty cell means the company does not publish that metric; no similar figure is substituted in its place.
